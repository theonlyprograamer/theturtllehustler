Before you dive in

Common questions

Sign up, verify your phone number and ID, and you can claim your first task within minutes — most hustlers get their first match the same day.

Posters fund the task upfront and it's held until the work is confirmed done. Hustlers get paid out straight to UPI the moment it's released — no chasing anyone for money.

Most legal, local, day-to-day work is fair game. A few categories need extra verification (home entry, childcare, high-value deliveries) to keep both sides safe.

Posting is free. A small service fee is taken from the task payout, shown upfront before a hustler accepts — no surprise deductions later.
